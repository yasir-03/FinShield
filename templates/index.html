About FinShield AI

Financial literacy shouldn't be a privilege.

Millions of people make everyday financial decisions — saving, spending, borrowing, investing — without the knowledge to do so safely. At the same time, digital banking and UPI adoption have opened the door to sophisticated cyber fraud that targets the financially unaware.

FinShield AI bridges that gap. Built as a Community Engagement Project, it combines structured learning modules, interactive quizzes, real calculators, an AI chatbot, and anonymous community surveys to both educate individuals and generate insights into a community's financial literacy and fraud exposure.

  • Beginner-friendly, example-driven content
  • Real-world fraud scenarios and prevention tips
  • Data-driven community insights via analytics

Fraud Awareness

Digital fraud is growing fast. Awareness is your best defense.

Scammers exploit trust, urgency, and confusion — not just technical loopholes. Recognizing the patterns is often enough to stop an attack before it starts.

Learn Cyber Security Basics
Phishing

Fake emails/websites impersonating your bank to steal credentials.

Vishing

Scam calls pretending to be bank staff asking for OTP or PIN.

QR Code Scams

Scanning a code to "receive" money that actually authorizes a payment out.

Screen Sharing

Fraudsters trick victims into installing remote-access apps.

Banks NEVER ask for your PIN, OTP or CVV over call, SMS or email. Report fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
